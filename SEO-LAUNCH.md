## Google Search Console
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and add the Domain property buildwithbridge.dev.
2. Copy Google's exact verification TXT record into your domain's DNS settings and verify ownership.
3. Submit sitemap.xml in the Sitemaps section.
4. Inspect the homepage and new pages using URL Inspection; use the live test and request indexing where appropriate.
5. Check Page indexing and Performance reports after Google processes the site. Submission does not guarantee indexing or rankings.
