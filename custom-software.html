Built around your process.

A spreadsheet that has become difficult to manage, a task entered into several systems, or project information scattered across folders can be a useful starting point. We review how your team works and identify what a focused application should do.

  • Features scoped to your actual workflow
  • Layouts designed for the devices your team uses
  • Accounts and access appropriate to the project
  • Integrations assessed before implementation
  • Testing and review before launch
  • Hosting and support planned with you

From an idea to a working application.

  1. Discuss your process, users, and the problem you want to solve.
  2. Define the features, data, integrations, and project scope.
  3. Build a first version and review it with your team.
  4. Test the agreed workflows and refine the application.
  5. Launch, complete onboarding, and agree on ongoing support.

We can start with a focused version and discuss additional features as your needs develop.

Common custom software questions.

Can you build something for my specific industry?

Tell us about your industry and workflow. We’ll review the requirements and explain what we can build, what needs further investigation, and how the project should be scoped.

Can it connect to tools we already use?

We assess the available APIs, permissions, and data access for those systems before promising an integration. Any limitations and third-party costs are included in planning.

Can we start small?

Yes. A focused first version can address the core workflow, with additional features scoped separately.

What happens after launch?

We agree on hosting, maintenance, and support requirements as part of the proposal. Ongoing changes and new features are scoped according to your needs.

What would make your work easier?

Tell us about the process you’d like to improve. Conversation and ideas are always free.
